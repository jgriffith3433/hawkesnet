Hawkes Net

Everyone on this page with the same room plays together. Move with the arrow keys, or drag or tap in the arena — touch a glowing orb to score. The host runs sixty-second rounds (shared timer, then a winner and scoreboard, then a fresh round). Small rooms go peer to peer; past 8 players the room moves to the relay without anyone reconnecting.

On a phone: press and drag in the lower half of the arena — a floating stick appears. Timer and your score sit in the safe area at the top.

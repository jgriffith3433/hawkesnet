Players find each other.

Hawkes Net is the multiplayer service behind every Hawkes game, open to outside developers too. It hands out tickets, keeps the room directory and runs the relay. Players talk directly to each other when their network allows it, and through the relay when it doesn't. You don't choose; the SDK does.

How it works

Tickets

A game asks for a ticket for a room. Tickets are signed, so a relay checks one without calling anyone.

Rooms

Players meet in named rooms. The directory shows who is where, and the SDK's lobby does the finding.

Peer to peer, then relay

The first player hosts when they can; if a link won't open, the room moves to the relay and says so.
